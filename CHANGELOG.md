# Changelog

All notable changes to Ember. Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-14

The first release.

### Fasting
- A fasting timer drawn as a ring of eight coloured stages, from digestion through fat burning, ketosis and autophagy to deep renewal at 72 hours. The current stage, the time to the next one and a short "right now" note for every hour of the fast.
- Plans from a gentle 12:12 to 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, one meal a day, 24, 36, 48 and 72 hours, or any length of your own.
- A daily schedule that shows your eating window.
- Start a fast you forgot to log, fix the start or end time, add past fasts, or discard one started by mistake.
- When a fast ends: the stages reached, how it felt and a note. Confetti when the goal is reached.

### Staying on track
- A streak that counts every day you reach your goal.
- Levels that grow with every hour fasted, from Spark to Sun.
- 33 badges for long fasts, streaks, total hours, weight, water, check-ins and hunger waves ridden out.
- A weekly challenge that changes every Monday.
- A tip of the day.

### Body and mind
- Hunger SOS: a 15-minute "ride the wave" timer with a breathing circle, and ten things that help right now.
- Check-ins for hunger, energy and mood.
- A daily water counter.
- Weight with a chart, goal weight and BMI.
- Progress: hours fasted per week or month, a calendar, stats and the full history.

### Learn
- The eight stages explained, with what helps in each.
- 18 short, honest articles: what breaks a fast, electrolytes, how to break a fast, exercise, sleep, fasting for women, longer fasts, safety and more.

### Notifications and widget
- A quiet timer in the notification shade while you fast, with an End fast button.
- Notifications when the goal is reached and when each new stage begins.
- A nudge at your scheduled start, with a Start now button, and a heads-up before the eating window closes.
- A home-screen widget with a live timer and progress.

### Everything else
- English and Russian.
- Light and dark themes.
- Kilograms or pounds.
- JSON backup.
- No account, no ads, no internet permission.
